Beyond the screen — a foil label

TRACE
Sea Serpent
Eau de parfum · 20% · Batch 008
Why foil fits TRACE. Hot-foil stamping is a print finish as well as a screen effect. The same iridescent wordmark could appear as a small foil detail on the collectible card or the sleeve — the apps and the product would then share one signature. On the label itself, the settled direction is blind embossing; foil would be a deliberate, separate decision, not a default.

How it's used here: foil only on brand moments — the wordmark, the primary action, selected chips, card edges. Never behind body text, and dark ink on every foil fill stays above 8:1.
