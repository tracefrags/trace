How to read an entry

Tags. BIOSHOP is from the supplier's product page. YOUR LIBRARY is from your Studio data. COMPOSE is from the conflict checker. GENERAL KNOWLEDGE is standard perfumery knowledge written by Claude — useful context, but not supplier data.


Neat vs stock. BioShop's usage table is the dose of the neat material as a % of your concentrate. If you hold a 10% stock, weigh ten times that figure of the stock. Entries show both.


Blotter strength. Decided by how far the bottle sits above its own top use level — not by whether it arrived pre-diluted. Under 30× smell as is; 30–120× dilute 1:9; over 120× dilute 1:99.


Position, tenacity, sillage. Position says when a material is noticed (top 0–30 min, heart 30 min–3 h, base 3 h+). Tenacity is how long it lasts; sillage is how far it projects. A material can last all day and still sit close to the skin.


Effective strength. A 10% stock is 90% carrier. Formulas built from many stocks hold less real aroma than their percentage suggests — Compose shows the effective figure.
